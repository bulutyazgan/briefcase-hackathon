# Brightline Studio (sample company onboarded into Tally)

Brightline Studio is a small web studio. We design and build websites for growing businesses.

## Services and price bands
- Landing page: £800 to £2,500, one-off.
- Multi-page site: £2,500 to £8,000, one-off.
- Website refresh: £300 to £800, one-off.
- Website care plan (hosting, updates, small edits, monitoring): £500 per month billed monthly, or £5,000 per year billed yearly (two months free).

## Terms
- 50% upfront for one-off projects, 7-day payment terms.
- Clients can switch the care plan between monthly and yearly at any time, starting from the next bill.

## Tone
Friendly, short, plain English. No jargon. Always confirm the amount before you invoice.
