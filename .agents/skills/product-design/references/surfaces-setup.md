# Approval surface

Load when reviewing or changing an approval card or its response flow.

Read `PLAN.md` and the owning feature before changing the surface.

## Review information

- Show what is being approved, the amount, and the policy reason.
- Keep supporting evidence available before the decision.
- Make approve and reject actions distinct.
- Show the pending state while the decision is being saved.
- Confirm the result and explain what happens next.

## Coverage

Check pending, approved, rejected, and failed responses. Preserve the decision
context if a request fails.
