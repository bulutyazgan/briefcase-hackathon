# Invoice and evidence surface

Load when reviewing invoice cards, invoice details, or evidence presentation.

Read `PLAN.md` and the `invoices` feature before changing the surface.

## Evidence

- Keep the amount, client, and invoice status easy to find.
- Show each line item with the evidence that supports it.
- Keep quotes verbatim. Distinguish memory facts from policy clauses.
- Link to the hosted invoice and the evidence PDF when they exist.
- Do not imply that an invoice was paid until the system reports that state.

## Coverage

Check empty, pending, paid, blocked, and error states where the feature can
reach them. Check long quotes and narrow viewports.
